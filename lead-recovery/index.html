How it works

Set it up once. It keeps working.

  1. 1

    Hand over your list

    Send the estimates from the last few months that never closed. A spreadsheet, a photo of your notebook, an export from your booking software. We clean it up.

  2. 2

    Approve the messages

    We write a short, friendly sequence in your voice. You read it, tweak it and approve it before a single text goes out.

  3. 3

    Replies come to you

    "Yes, let's do it" gets a booking link and an alert to your phone. "Not now" gets a reminder later. "Stop" is removed right away.

  4. 4

    New quotes join automatically

    Every new estimate that goes quiet gets the same follow-up, so nothing slips again.
