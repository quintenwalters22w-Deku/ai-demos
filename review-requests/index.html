How it works

Finish the job. The asking happens on its own.

  1. 1

    Job marked done

    When a job is finished in your calendar, invoice or booking tool (or you just text a number), the customer is queued up.

  2. 2

    A friendly check-in

    At the time you pick, they get a short thank-you text from your business asking how the job went, 1 to 5.

  3. 3

    Happy? Review link.

    4s and 5s get a one-tap link to your review page with a simple "would you mind sharing that?" No login hunting.

  4. 4

    Not happy? You first.

    1s to 3s get a "sorry, the owner will call you" reply and you get an instant alert, so you can fix it before it turns into a public 1-star.

Every customer can still leave a public review any time. We never block or filter reviews; we just make sure you hear about problems early.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

Will customers feel spammed?

No. Each customer gets one thank-you check-in per job and, if they're happy, one link. No reminder chains, no marketing blasts.

Is it allowed to send unhappy customers to me first?

Yes, as long as nobody is blocked from posting. Every customer can still leave a public review whenever they want. This just gives you the chance to make it right first.

How does it know a job is done?

It can watch your calendar, invoices or booking software, or you can text a customer's name and number to a private number when you wrap up. We pick whatever you already do.

Can I change the wording?

Anytime. Tell us and it's updated. Changes are included in the monthly plan.

What if someone texts STOP?

They're removed right away and never texted again.
