    FAQ

    Questions owners ask

    Will callers know it's AI?

    It introduces itself as your business's virtual assistant. Most callers just care that someone picked up and booked them.

    Do I need a new phone number?

    No. Your current number stays. Missed or after-hours calls forward to the receptionist.

    What if it doesn't know an answer?

    It takes a detailed message, tells the caller you'll follow up, and texts you right away.

    Can I change what it says?

    Yes. Updates to hours, prices or scripts are covered by the $99/mo retainer.

    How long is the contract?

    Plans run 6 months or a full year. We'll talk through which fits on the demo call.
