How it works

  1. Answers instantly

    Trained on your services, service area, hours and policies, so it answers the way your office would.

  2. Qualifies the job

    Asks what's wrong, the roof type, the size and how urgent it is. No more "call me back" with zero details.

  3. Gives a safe ballpark

    Shares a range you set, always framed as "final price after inspection," so nobody gets surprised.

  4. Books and hands off

    Offers open inspection slots, then texts you a lead card with everything you need to call back.

What the owner gets

  • A text with the visitor's name, need, roof details and chosen time
  • Emergency leaks flagged to the top
  • A daily summary of every chat, booked or not
  • Answers you can edit anytime, with no tech skills needed

FAQ

Will it give prices I don't agree with?

No. It only shares the ranges you approve, and always says the final price comes after an inspection.

What if someone has an emergency leak?

It flags the chat as urgent, shares your emergency line and texts you right away.

Does it work on my current website?

Yes. It's a small chat bubble that can be added to most websites in under a day.

What does it cost?

$500 setup plus $99/month, which covers maintenance and updates to its answers.
