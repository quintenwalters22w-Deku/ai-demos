How it's figured

The formula, in the open

Same approach as Keystone's public answering-services index, which models annual lost revenue per trade as:

calls per week × miss rate × 46% × average job value × 52 weeks

This calculator lets you edit every input, including the 46% (shown as "% who'd become paying jobs"). The extra "% who never call back" field is set to 100% by default, which leaves the formula unchanged. Monthly = yearly ÷ 12.

Sources

  • Keystone, answering-services research (formula; Invoca 27% unanswered and 46% conversion figures): keystoneapp.io/research/answering-services
  • CallRail (Jan 2025, 1.1M conversations): up to 85% of unanswered callers never call back, as reported in this guide: automateline.com/guides/missed-call-text-back-for-hvac-companies
