Common questions

Are chemicals included?

Yes. Every weekly plan includes standard chlorine, acid and stabilizer. Specialty treatments like phosphate remover are quoted first (example policy).

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave the gate unlocked or share a code. You'll get a photo and readings after every visit.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Service is month to month and you can pause or cancel with a week's notice.

What happens after a monsoon storm?

Weekly customers get a free storm check within 48 hours for debris and chemical rebalance (example policy).

Pool turned green after the storm?

Send a photo and we'll text back a recovery price, usually within the hour.
