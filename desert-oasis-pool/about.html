Pool people, born in the desert

Desert Oasis started with one truck and a simple promise: show up on the same day every week, and prove it with a photo. That promise still runs the whole company.

Why we do it this way

Most pool complaints come down to two things: not knowing if the tech came, and surprise bills. So every visit ends with a photo and readings sent to your phone, and every repair gets a written price before we start.

Our techs are trained on the equipment Tucson pools actually use, from variable-speed pumps to salt systems, and they carry the common parts on the truck.
