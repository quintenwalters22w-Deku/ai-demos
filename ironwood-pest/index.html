How the perimeter plan works

One barrier, renewed every season

  1. Inspect. A licensed tech walks the yard, block wall, garage and attic access and shows you what they find.
  2. Treat & seal. Interior baseboards, exterior foundation band, eaves and weep screeds. Gaps under doors get noted or sealed.
  3. Renew quarterly. We come back each season, timed to Tucson's pest calendar, and text you the day before.
  4. Free re-treats. See activity between visits? We come back at no charge.
