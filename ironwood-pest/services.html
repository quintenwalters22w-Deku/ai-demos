Common questions

Are your treatments safe for kids and pets?

We use products labeled for residential use and apply them where pests travel, not where kids play. We'll tell you how long to keep pets off treated areas, usually until dry.

Can you get rid of scorpions completely?

No honest company can promise zero scorpions in the Sonoran Desert. What we can do is greatly reduce what gets inside through regular treatment, blacklight hunts and sealing.

Do I need to sign a contract?

Perimeter plans are pay-per-visit with no long-term contract. Termite warranties renew yearly.

What if I see pests between visits?

Text or call. Re-treats between scheduled visits are free on any plan.

Found a scorpion in the bathtub?

Book a blacklight inspection tonight or tomorrow. Call, text, or send the form and we'll reply within minutes.
