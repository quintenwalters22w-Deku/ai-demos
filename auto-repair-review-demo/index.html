      Under the hood

      How it works

      1. 1

        Job closes

        When a repair is marked done or paid, the customer is queued. Repeat asks, opted-out numbers and flagged jobs are skipped.

      2. 2

        Thank-you + check-in

        A short thank-you text goes out during business hours, followed by a simple “How did we do? 1–5.”

      3. 3

        The right next step

        Happy customers get a one-tap link to your review page. Unhappy customers get a direct line to the owner first, and the public link is still there for everyone.

      4. 4

        One reminder, then done

        No reply? One friendly reminder later, then it stops. Replying STOP opts out for good. You see it all on the dashboard.

      Fair to everyone: every customer can leave a public review. Private follow-up is an extra, not a filter, so you stay within review-site rules. No discounts or gifts for reviews.
