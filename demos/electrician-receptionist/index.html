How it works

Set up once, answers forever

  1. 1

    We learn your shop

    Services, service area ZIPs, hours, on-call rules, the jobs you want and the ones you don't.

  2. 2

    Calls forward to it

    When you can't pick up, or after hours, the receptionist answers in a natural voice with your business name.

  3. 3

    It sorts and books

    Emergencies get safety steps and a page to your on-call tech. Everything else gets qualified and a slot on your calendar.

  4. 4

    You get one text

    Name, number, address, what they need, and what was promised. Spam and sales calls never reach you.
