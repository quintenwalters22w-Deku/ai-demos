Safety first

Is it safe to use my door?

My spring broke. Can I open the door by hand?

Please don't. Without a working spring the door can weigh 150+ lbs and drop suddenly. Leave it closed and call us; most spring jobs are done same day.

The door reverses right before it closes.

Usually the safety sensors near the floor are misaligned or dirty. Wipe the lenses and check both lights are solid. If it keeps happening, we can align or replace them.

Yes, we repair all common residential door and opener makes.
