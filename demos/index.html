Sample trade demos

Five interactive examples of tools a local service business can use. Nothing on these pages is live customer data, and none of the forms send anything.

  • Quote request form

    A three-step quote form for a sample plumbing shop, with an admin view of incoming leads.

  • Mini CRM

    A sample HVAC sales pipeline. Drag leads between stages and open a card for notes.

  • Review collector

    A post-job rating flow for a sample roofing shop, with a public testimonial wall and an admin review step.

  • Client portal

    A sample landscaping client portal: project progress, files, messages, and approvals.

  • Missed-call text-back

    A phone mockup showing how a missed call gets a text back in under five minutes, plus follow-up and STOP handling.
