How it works

Four steps, set up once

  1. 1

    A call goes unanswered

    You're under a dishwasher or it's 8 PM. The call rings out like it always has.

  2. 2

    A text goes out in seconds

    The customer gets a friendly text in your shop's name before they call the next company.

  3. 3

    It qualifies and books

    Appliance, brand, symptom and ZIP, a quick tip if food or water is at risk, then open slots from your calendar.

  4. 4

    You get the summary

    One text with name, number, problem and booked time. Replies of STOP are honored right away.
