How long does setup take?

It depends on what you pick and how quickly you can approve messages. A single automation is usually quicker than a full Pack or a website. [Quinten to confirm typical turnaround] I'll give you a real date on our call.

Does it work with my current phone number?

In most cases, yes. You keep your number and your customers keep calling the same number. How it connects depends on your phone carrier and setup, and I'll check that with you before you pay anything.

What happens when a customer replies to a text?

Their reply comes to you right away, by text, email, or both, so you can pick up the conversation yourself. Follow-ups stop once someone replies, so they don't get a canned message after they've already talked to you.

Will callers know they're talking to an automated assistant?

Yes. The phone assistant says up front that it's automated. It takes the caller's details, flags urgent jobs, and sends you a summary. It doesn't pretend to be you or your staff, and it doesn't quote prices or diagnose problems.

Is texting customers like this allowed?

Texts only go to people who contacted you or did business with you, like someone who just called your line or a customer whose job you finished. Every message includes an opt-out, and opt-outs are honored automatically. I also handle the business texting registration your number may need.

I'm not good with tech. How much do I have to do?

Not much. You tell me how you like to work, approve the messages, and answer a test call. I do the setup and the upkeep.

Do I need one of your websites to use the automations?

No. The automations work with your current number, and the chat assistant works on most existing websites. The website is there if you want one.
