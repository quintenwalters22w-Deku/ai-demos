How it works

Four steps. You do very little of the work.

Short call

We talk for 15 minutes or so about how calls and leads come in now, where they get dropped, and what you want fixed first. Price is on the table here too.

I set it up

I build it around your existing business number and the tools you already use. You approve every text and script before anything goes live.

We test it together

We call your line, miss a call on purpose, and send a test lead. You see exactly what your customers will see.

It runs, and I look after it

I keep an eye on it, fix what breaks, and make small changes when you need them. If you want to change something, text or email me.

Let's see what you're missing.
